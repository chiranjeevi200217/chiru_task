# LeadLens — 1:45 Video Demo Walkthrough Script
**Target Audience:** Caprae Capital / PE Search Fund Investment Committee

### [0:00 - 0:20] The Problem: Sourcing Noise in Search Funds
**[Visual: LeadLens Dashboard with 310 leads loaded, cursor hovering over raw domains and stats strip]**

> *"When a search fund or private equity firm uses tools like SaaSquatch to source small B2B SaaS targets, the bottleneck isn't finding domains—it's analyst time. Raw scraping dumps hundreds of unvetted inboxes, duplicate brand variants, and misaligned targets.*
> 
> *I built **LeadLens** to serve as the intelligent qualification layer between raw lead scraping and your CRM, evaluating targets against real ETA investment criteria."*

---

### [0:21 - 0:52] Feature 1: Acquisition-Fit Lead Scoring & AI Rationale
**[Visual: Click on top-ranked lead "AeroFlow", slide-over detail panel opens showing 6 sub-signals]**

> *"Feature 1 is explainable lead scoring from 0 to 100. We don't use a black box. Each lead is evaluated across six transparent signals: SaaS category defensibility, the 6-to-25 employee sweet spot, company vintage, revenue bracket, direct founder contact quality, and website health.*
> 
> *Every target receives an instant deal rationale generated by Claude 3.5—with a zero-latency deterministic PE fallback when offline.*
> 
> **[Visual: Click 'Tune Weights' button, drag 'Founder Direct Contact Quality' slider from 15% to 35%]**
> 
> *Search funds have different mandates. Opening our Calibrator panel allows analysts to adjust sliders or choose presets like 'Founder-First Outreach'—and watch the deal matrix re-rank in real time."*

---

### [0:53 - 1:22] Feature 2: Deduplication, MX Validation & CRM Export
**[Visual: Click '2. Clean-up & Dedupe' button in navbar, modal appears displaying duplicate clusters]**

> *"Feature 2 solves dirty data. Scraping frequently captures 'GetAcme.io' alongside 'app.acme.io' or fuzzy legal variants like 'Acme Technologies LLC'.*
> 
> *LeadLens runs canonical domain normalization paired with Sørensen-Dice fuzzy matching. In this review modal, analysts see side-by-side comparison cards and merge duplicate records with one click, preserving the best founder contact.*
> 
> **[Visual: Click 'Validate MX & Web' in navbar, then open 'Export CRM' modal]**
> 
> *Next, our validation pipeline verifies RFC email syntax, resolves domain MX records, and checks website HTTP health. When you're ready, click Export to generate a HubSpot-ready CSV with pre-mapped properties for instant outreach."*

### [1:23 - 1:45] Architecture, Ethics & Summary
**[Visual: Terminal showing 100% passing Vitest test suite, then scroll down README architecture diagram]**

> *"Under the hood: Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Prisma Postgres on Supabase, and Upstash Redis with a 24-hour TTL on DNS resolutions.*

> *For enrichment, we adhere strictly to ethical data standards: fetching only the public root homepage, obeying robots.txt, and broadcasting an identified User-Agent.*
